In plain English

Think of OpenAI as the Apple of AI — they don't show you the recipe, but they polish the experience until it just works. They invented the modern chatbot (ChatGPT) and still set the pace for what "AI assistant" feels like.

What you get: world-class general intelligence wrapped in a polished API. You send text (or image, audio), GPT-5 sends back text. o3 and o3 Pro are slower, more expensive cousins that think before answering — better for hard math, science, multi-step planning.

What you give up: control. You can't download the model, you can't audit it, your data flows through OpenAI's servers, and prices are the highest in the market.

Use OpenAI when: you need the best quality, you need ChatGPT integration, your team already knows the API, or you're building consumer-facing AI where polish matters.

Overview

OpenAI is an AI research company that pioneered the GPT architecture and launched ChatGPT in late 2022, sparking the current AI boom. Microsoft is its largest backer ($13B+ invested) and exclusive cloud partner.

Flagship models

  • GPT-5 — flagship multimodal
  • o3, o4-mini — reasoning models
  • DALL·E 3, Sora — image and video generation
  • Whisper — speech-to-text

Strengths

  • Best-in-class general reasoning and tool use
  • Largest developer ecosystem
  • ChatGPT has 500M+ weekly users

Architecture & scale

  • Parameters: GPT-5 estimated 1.8T+ (MoE); o-series uses test-time reasoning
  • Training compute: 10²⁵ – 10²⁶ FLOPs class
  • Context window: 400K (GPT-5) to 1.05M (GPT-5.5)
  • Multimodal: text, image, audio, function calling, structured outputs
  • Reasoning models use hidden chain-of-thought tokens (billed)

Benchmarks (May 2026)

  • MMLU-Pro: ~85% (GPT-5)
  • SWE-bench Verified: ~68% (GPT-5.2-Codex)
  • GPQA Diamond: ~88% (o3 Pro)
  • ARC-AGI v2: ~75% (o3 Pro w/ high compute)
  • LMSYS Arena: consistently top-3

Notable customers

  • Microsoft (deepest partnership)
  • Apple Intelligence (on-device)
  • Salesforce, Stripe, Klarna, Morgan Stanley
  • US federal agencies via Azure Government

Funding & valuation

  • Total raised: $60B+ including Microsoft's $13B
  • Valuation: $500B (Oct 2024 secondary), targeting $1T+ in 2026
  • Revenue: ~$12B ARR (Q1 2026)
  • Burn: $5B+ in 2024; profitable on inference, loss-making on training

Recent milestones

  • GPT-5 launch (Q2 2025)
  • For-profit conversion finalized (2025)
  • Sora 2 with 4K + audio (late 2025)
  • Operator browser agent launched (Jan 2025)
  • Stargate $500B infrastructure JV with Oracle/SoftBank (2025)

Limitations & risks

  • Highest API costs in the market for frontier tier
  • Strict rate limits and content policies
  • Closed weights — cannot self-host or audit
  • Heavy Microsoft dependency creates concentration risk
  • Governance turmoil ongoing (board crisis 2023, executive churn 2024-25)

Leadership

  • Sam Altman — CEO
  • Greg Brockman — President
  • Mira Murati — left 2024 (founded Thinking Machines)
  • Ilya Sutskever — left 2024 (founded Safe Superintelligence)
