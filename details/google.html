In plain English

Think of Google's AI as the everything-store. They have the most resources, the most data (Search, YouTube, Gmail), their own chips (TPUs), and they ship the widest catalog of models.

What you get: Gemini 3.1 Pro with the industry's biggest context window (2 million tokens — that's like the entire Lord of the Rings trilogy in one prompt). Native multimodal — it understands video, audio, and images directly. Veo 3 generates video with synchronized audio. And there's a free open variant (Gemma) you can self-host.

What you give up: Slightly less polish for developer workflows compared to Claude/OpenAI. Confusing branding (Bard → Gemini → multiple variants).

Use Gemini when: you need to process long documents/video, you're already on Google Cloud, you want native audio in/out, or you need the cheapest enterprise-grade flagship (Gemini 3.1 Pro is half the price of GPT-5.5).

Overview

Google merged Brain and DeepMind in 2023. Builds the Gemini family plus AlphaFold, AlphaProteo.

Flagship models

  • Gemini 3.1 Pro — 2M context, multimodal
  • Gemini 2.5 Flash — fast and cheap
  • Gemma 3 — open-weight smaller models
  • Veo 3, Imagen 3 — video and image

Strengths

  • Integration with Search, Workspace, Android
  • Native multimodality from day one
  • Owns TPU silicon

Architecture & scale

  • Gemini 3.1 Pro: sparse MoE, est. 1.5T+ params
  • Context window: 2M tokens (industry leader); 10M experimental
  • Native multimodal: text, image, audio in/out, video
  • Trained on TPU v5p/v6 — Google's custom silicon advantage
  • Gemma 3 open variant: 2B/9B/27B params, Apache 2.0

Benchmarks (May 2026)

  • MMLU-Pro: ~86% (Gemini 3.1 Pro)
  • MMMU (multimodal): #1 across most categories
  • Video understanding: Best-in-class for long video (90+ min)
  • Needle-in-haystack: ~99% accuracy at 2M tokens
  • AlphaProteo — designed novel proteins (Nature 2024)

Notable products & customers

  • Powers Google Search AI Overviews (~2B users)
  • Gmail, Docs, Sheets, Meet, Android, YouTube
  • Waymo autonomous driving
  • Snap, Spotify, Wayfair, Wendy's
  • 27% of Fortune 500 use Gemini via Vertex AI

Funding & financials

  • Alphabet capex: $75B in 2025, ~$100B planned for 2026 (mostly AI infrastructure)
  • AI revenue: Cloud AI ~$15B ARR; consumer AI bundled in Google One
  • Stock impact: Gemini momentum drove Alphabet to $2.5T+ market cap

Recent milestones

  • Gemini 3 launch (Q1 2026)
  • AlphaFold 3 — atomic-level structure prediction (2024)
  • Veo 3 with native synchronized audio (late 2025)
  • Project Astra real-time AI assistant (rolled out 2025)
  • TPU v6 Trillium GA (2025)

Limitations & risks

  • Slower to ship vs. OpenAI/Anthropic in dev workflows
  • Brand confusion (Bard → Gemini rebrand, multiple model variants)
  • Antitrust scrutiny on search-AI integration
  • Less developer mindshare for coding
