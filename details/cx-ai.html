In plain English

Customer support has been transformed. The new generation (Sierra, Decagon, Cresta) doesn't just answer FAQs — they actually resolve tickets by taking actions: issuing refunds, updating accounts, troubleshooting via tool calls. Resolution rates of 50-80% autonomous are now common.

Sierra (founded by Bret Taylor, ex-Salesforce co-CEO) charges outcome-based ($1-2 per resolved conversation) — you only pay when it works. Customers include Sonos, ADT, WeightWatchers.

Resolution rates

Modern AI CX agents resolve 50-80% of inbound tickets autonomously. Best deployments hit 90%+. Quality often matches or beats human agents per CSAT.
